How to tune your new guitar

Standard tuning begins with the lowest string, low E, and ends with the highest, high E. Start by turning the low E string to the proper pitch. Then continue this pattern for all strings.

All about playing chords

The most common chords include major chords which are G, C, and D. These are bright and happy. Minor chords, like Am, Em, and Dm, are known for their emotional tones.
